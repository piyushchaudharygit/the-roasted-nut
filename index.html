Our story

A place that feels like home

The Roasted Nuts is a cosy café where freshly baked treats, quick bites, desserts, and refreshing drinks come together beautifully under one roof.

Whether you’re stopping in for a crispy samosa and a cold coffee, grabbing a slice of cake to celebrate, or simply looking for a warm and welcoming spot to unwind — we’ve got something for every craving and every mood.

Find us at Ganga Nagar, Sarojini Nagar, Lucknow. Everything we serve is made with genuine care — because food tastes better when it’s prepared with love.
